# Week 2: Batches, Streams & Screams

**Topic:** Batch vs streaming, ETL vs ELT  
**Status:** scaffold. The exercise lands here when this week's article is out.

## What's coming

Two decisions shape almost every pipeline. First, timing: load on a schedule in chunks (batch), or process events as they arrive (streaming). Second, order: transform the data on the way in (ETL) or land it raw and transform it inside the warehouse (ELT). Week 2 walks through both, what each choice costs, and why Week 1's freshness question settles more of it than people expect.
